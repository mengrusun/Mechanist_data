Closest prior work: The reasoning/rule-vs-memorization machinery this proposal needs largely exists. LiReFs ("The Reasoning-Memorization Interplay Is Mediated by a Single Direction", arXiv 2503.23084, 2025) already finds a SINGLE linear direction that switches between reasoning/rule and memorization pathways and can be causally steered — essentially the "rule pathway" knob the proposal wants to suppress. "Reason to Rote" (2025) shows intermediate reasoning outputs are computed even when a memorized label is emitted (rule and exception coexist and compete). "Disentangling Recall and Reasoning" / "Decoupling Knowledge and Reasoning" (2025) show separable-but-interacting circuits/layers. Computational-linguistics work ("Exceptions, Instantiations, and Overgeneralization: How LMs Process Generics", CL 2024) already studies overgeneralization/exception handling in LMs. Classic cognitive science (past-tense debate, U-shaped overregularization, "goed"/"feeled") is the analogy the proposal borrows. So both the phenomenon analogy and the separable rule/memory circuits with a steerable switch direction are established; the proposal does not discover them. Its delta: (1) name "overregularization" as a concrete, predictable FACTUAL-answering failure mode — the rule pathway overrides a CORRECTLY-STORED exception — and demonstrate it across three domains (irregular morphology, factual relations with statistical defaults like capital!=largest-city / founder!=CEO, numeric/temporal regularities); (2) predict errors concentrate on LOW-FREQUENCY exceptions with strong rule support (above frequency/similarity-matched baselines) and that wrong answers are disproportionately the EXACT rule-predicted value; (3) show suppressing the RULE pathway (not amplifying the fact, and unlike ROME which edits the fact) selectively recovers the stored exception while preserving correct rule application on true regulars. The novel pieces are the cross-domain overregularization failure-mode taxonomy tied to exception frequency/rule strength and the rule-suppression-as-fix contrast with fact editing; the underlying separable pathways and single switch direction are borrowed (esp. LiReFs). Who cares: interpretability (reasoning vs memorization), knowledge-editing, factual-reliability, and cog-sci/psycholinguistics-adjacent audiences. Main risk: the "rule pathway" may just be the already-known LiReFs reasoning/memorization direction, making the intervention incremental; and separating true overregularization from ordinary wrong-answer or the model simply not storing the exception requires strong storage-verification, which is a hard confound.
