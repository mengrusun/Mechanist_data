Closest prior work: The metacognition/knowledge-boundary literature is crowded and recent. "LLMs Know When They Know... A Metacognitive Harness" (2026) explicitly imports Nelson-Narens theory and separates a PRE-solve feeling-of-knowing (FOK) from post-solve judgment-of-learning — i.e., the pre-retrieval familiarity estimate is already named and studied behaviorally. "How Knowledge Popularity Influences Knowledge Boundary Perception" (2025) shows popularity (esp. relation popularity) drives boundary perception/confidence — the surface-cue driver this proposal posits. "Causal Evidence that Language Models use Confidence to Drive Behavior" (2026) and confidence-regulation-neurons work show activation-steering of an internal confidence signal causally shifts abstention rates — i.e., causal confidence-gating of answer/abstain is established. Answerability/(un)answerability directions are linearly readable mid-layer and steerable (Slobodkin 2023; 2025 work). "Knowledge Knows, Verbalization Tells" (2026) disentangles latent solvability from verbalization directions. So the pieces — a pre-retrieval FOK, popularity as its driver, causal confidence steering of abstention, and content-vs-confidence probe separation — largely exist. The proposal's delta: (a) frame the boundary signal as TWO dissociable computations, a surface-cue familiarity/coverage estimate vs the actual retrieved content, with familiarity emerging at an EARLIER layer and computed before/independently of content; (b) a symmetric two-failure account where false-HIGH familiarity -> confident confabulation with little retrieved content and false-LOW familiarity -> suppressed retrieval of a stored fact ("give-up" abstention); (c) recalibrating familiarity ALONE (leaving stored facts untouched) fixes BOTH failure modes with higher specificity than confidence-threshold abstention or ROME. The genuinely novel piece is the explicit familiarity-vs-content dissociation with layer-onset ordering and the unified symmetric-failure recalibration; the FOK concept, popularity driver, and confidence-steers-abstention causality are largely known. Who cares: metacognition/calibration, hallucination-abstention, retrieval-gating, reliability communities. Main risk: "familiarity vs content" may be hard to separate from the existing confidence-vs-correctness / solvability-vs-verbalization probe distinctions, and the "computed before content" claim (layer onset) is the crux that could collapse novelty if content is also decodable early.
