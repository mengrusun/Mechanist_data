Closest prior work: The "retrieval as competition / interference" framing has real precedents. "Forbidden Facts" (2023) decomposed Llama-2 and found ~35 suppressor components that can suppress a competing/forbidden token — direct evidence of winner-take-all competition and distractor suppression circuitry. "Summing Up The Facts" (2024) shows factual recall is an additive sum of component contributions to logits via the unembedding (the exact logit-lens readout this proposal uses). "Transformers Remember First, Forget Last" and proactive-interference / working-memory-limit papers (2025-26) frame recall failures as retrieval-based interference among competing traces. On the failure-typing side, "LLMs Do NOT Really Know What They Don't Know" (2025) shows true facts and associated hallucinations share the same recall geometry (internal signals track retrieval activity, not truth), and hallucination taxonomies (DECK, DINCO's distractor-normalized confidence) already try to separate overconfident fabrication from genuine ignorance. So the ingredients — competitor readout via unembedding, suppressor mechanisms, interference framing, failure-type taxonomies — all exist. The proposal's delta: (a) explicitly read the *competitor-set geometry* (number of viable competitors + margin between top distractor and correct answer) from intermediate layers and use it to predict failure TYPE (interference-driven wrong answer vs. genuine ignorance), a distinction current confidence probes conflate; (b) show hidden-knowledge failures are disproportionately interference-driven; (c) recover correct answers by SUBTRACTING the winning distractor's direction, claimed to be more selective than amplifying the correct answer or ROME editing. Who cares: interpretability, hallucination-detection/reliability, knowledge-editing communities. Novelty is moderate — the competition mechanism is partly established (Forbidden Facts), but using competitor geometry as a failure-TYPE classifier and distractor-subtraction as a targeted recovery lever, tied to the hidden-knowledge gap, is a reasonable non-trivial synthesis. Main risk: distractor-subtraction may reduce to logit-difference steering already known, and separating interference from ignorance may be confounded by the shared-recall-geometry finding.
