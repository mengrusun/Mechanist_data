SEARCH SUMMARY:
Closest prior work: This proposal is tightly built on a very recent, specific finding it cites — Chen et al. 2025, "The Structure of Relation Decoding Linear Operators" (arXiv 2510.26543), which shows linear relation decoders (LREs; Hernandez et al. 2023) are PROPERTY-CENTRIC: they extract a coarse shared property (e.g., a country subspace) reused across semantically-close "sibling" relations rather than capturing fine-grained relation-specific mappings, explaining limited relation-wise generalization. "On Relation-Specific Neurons" (EMNLP 2025, arXiv 2502.17355) reports neuron VERSATILITY and INTERFERENCE — deactivating one relation's neurons changes another's recall, i.e., sibling-relation confusion at the neuron level already observed. So the shared-property geometry and cross-relation interference are established; the proposal does not discover the shared structure. Its delta: (1) name and quantify "property collapse" as a concrete, predictable factual FAILURE MODE — errors concentrate on relations sharing a property with a competing relation, and wrong answers are disproportionately VALID attributes of a sibling relation for the same subject (above frequency/similarity-matched baselines); (2) causally DECOMPOSE each relation's decoder into a shared-property component + a relation-specific residual via LREs, showing patching the shared component transfers answers across siblings while the residual carries the disambiguating signal, and collapse errors correspond to a weak residual; (3) a low-rank "relation-disambiguation" steering vector amplifying the residual recovers correct attributes more selectively than ROME. The failure-mode framing, the shared-vs-residual causal decomposition tied to error prevalence, and the residual-sharpening fix are the novel, non-trivial contributions; the underlying property-centric geometry is borrowed. Who cares: interpretability (relation representation), knowledge-editing, factual-reliability communities. Main risk: "property collapse" may be a re-description of already-documented sibling interference; the LRE decomposition into shared + residual is a natural next step from Chen et al., so novelty rides on whether the residual-sharpening fix is genuinely more selective than editing and whether the error-concentration prediction holds above matched baselines.
