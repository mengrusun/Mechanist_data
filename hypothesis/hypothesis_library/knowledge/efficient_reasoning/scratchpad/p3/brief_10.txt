Closest prior work: heavy recent literature on multilingual reasoning gaps and English-pivoted CoT (Why Do Multilingual Reasoning Gaps Emerge 2025; Long CoT Across Languages 2508.14828; Cross-lingual Collapse 2506.05850; Do Llamas Work in English 2024). It is established that (a) LRMs route concepts through an English-centered latent space even for non-English prompts, (b) lower token-activation-rate / low-resource target languages elicit MORE reasoning tokens as compensation (EfficientXLang 2507.00246), and (c) ablating/steering a single activation direction can reduce overthinking (Manifold Steering 2505.22411).
Actual delta: The proposal's novelty is reframing the extra non-English tokens as a causally-removable "pivot detour" OVERHEAD (not genuine reasoning) tied to a language-specific representational component, and claiming token cuts with NO accuracy loss via mid-generation ablation. The English-pivot phenomenon, the extra-token phenomenon, and single-direction overthinking ablation all individually exist; the specific causal decomposition (overhead vs genuine reasoning) and the no-accuracy-loss ablation of a language-pivot component mid-generation is the delta, but it sits close to Manifold Steering + EfficientXLang combined.
Who cares: multilingual NLP, efficient-reasoning, equity/fairness for low-resource-language users (a real "language tax" cost argument). Likely lands at an *ACL/EMNLP or interpretability workshop if the causal no-accuracy-loss claim holds. Risk: the "overhead is not genuine reasoning" claim is hard to isolate cleanly and prior work already shows compensation tokens partly help accuracy.
