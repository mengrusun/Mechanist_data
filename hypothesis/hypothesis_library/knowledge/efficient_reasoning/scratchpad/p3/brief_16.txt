Closest prior work: STRONG and direct overlap. "Think Deep, Not Just Long: Measuring LLM Reasoning Effort via Deep-Thinking Tokens" (2602.13517) already finds that higher reasoning levels REDISTRIBUTE computation between per-token depth and sequence length — "higher reasoning levels require less deep revision per token but generate longer chains," i.e., a depth<->length trade in the OPPOSITE direction from the proposal. Token Complexity (2503.01141, ICML 2025, the proposal's anchor) establishes the information-theoretic per-problem token floor and universal length-accuracy tradeoff, but treats computation as living in token space only. Per-token computation-density estimators already exist (MTD / Divergence Steering; "Sparse or Dense?"; deep-thinking-token metric), as does recurrent-depth/latent-reasoning and pause/padding-token work (adding depth without tokens). "Reasoning as Compression / Conditional Information Bottleneck" (2603.08462) frames budget-forcing as compression.
Actual delta: The proposal's specific claim is the CONVERSE causal direction and a conservation law: when a model is FORCED/steered to be terse, it partially COMPENSATES by increasing per-token computation depth on surviving tokens, this compensation is measurable per-token, SATURATES at a ceiling, and the ceiling/failure point PREDICTS where compression breaks accuracy. Prior work shows the depth<->length tradeoff exists and has density estimators, but frames it as a property of reasoning LEVEL, not as a compensation mechanism triggered by forced compression with a predictive breaking point. The delta is thin — the core "conservation of compute between depth and length" is largely established by Think-Deep-Not-Just-Long; the added value is the forced-compression causal test + saturation-ceiling-predicts-accuracy-breakdown claim.
Who cares: efficient-reasoning, test-time-compute theory, model-compression/latent-reasoning. Strong "so what" if the saturation ceiling PREDICTS the compression-breaking point (an actionable compression limit). Risk: heavy overlap with recent depth-vs-length work means novelty is the main vulnerability; the per-token density measurement (KL to shallow head) is a known, noisy proxy.
