=== SEARCH SUMMARY ===
Closest prior work: two literatures the proposal bridges. (1) Cognitive science on analogical transfer (Gentner Structure-Mapping Theory / Structure-Mapping Engine; Surface vs Structural Similarity in Analogical Transfer, Holyoak/Gick) — humans reuse solutions to structurally-similar problems, and transfer fails when only surface (not structural) similarity is present. Recent LLM analogy work confirms LLMs succeed on near/surface analogies but fail far/structural ones (GPT-4 false analogies are driven by surface similarity; Mutual Alignment Score tracks success). (2) Interpretability of in-context task reuse: task vectors, concept vectors in a few attention heads that linearly encode analogical relations and causally steer behavior (In-Context Analogical Reasoning; task-vector work), Circuit Component Reuse Across Tasks, Function Induction, Just-in-time/Distributed Task Representations.
Actual delta: The proposal's specific claim is (a) a NEW efficiency phenomenon: reasoning models shown a fully-worked structurally-identical example immediately before a target FREQUENTLY FAIL to reuse the method and re-derive from scratch, wasting many tokens; and (b) a MECHANISM: an internal, linearly-decodable "analogy/structure-mapping gate" signal that fires (or fails) at the moment the target begins and CAUSALLY determines transfer vs re-derivation — steering it flips the model between reuse and re-derivation, mirroring the human surface-vs-structural modulation. Task/concept vectors and circuit reuse exist, and surface-vs-structural failure is documented behaviorally in analogy QA; the delta is framing this as a TOKEN-EFFICIENCY / overthinking failure in reasoning models with a causal onset gate. Genuine but built from known parts; the "reasoning models waste tokens re-deriving a just-shown solution" phenomenon is the freshest piece.
Who cares: efficient-reasoning, in-context-learning/analogy interpretability, cognitive-science-of-LLMs, prompt design (few-shot worked examples). Strong "so what": if models fail to reuse a just-given worked solution, few-shot/worked-example prompting is leaving large token savings on the table, and a steerable gate recovers them. Risk: cand 17 and cand 18 are near-duplicates; the causal-gate claim overlaps heavily with existing task-vector steering; isolating a dedicated "analogy gate" distinct from generic task-vector formation is the crux.
