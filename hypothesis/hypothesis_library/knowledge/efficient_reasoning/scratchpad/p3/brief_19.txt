Closest prior work: the efficient-reasoning-interpretability cluster reads a SCALAR pre-generation signal (difficulty / eventual-success probability) and allocates a length/compute budget from it: "LLMs Encode Their Failures: Predicting Success from Pre-Generation Activations" (2602.09924, the proposal's anchor), difficulty-perception probes for routing, Temporal Predictors of Outcome / Knowing Before Saying (early correctness), Tracing the Traces (latent-trajectory trace selection), FR-Ponder / Learning to Ponder (learned scalar compute), and difficulty-aware allocation/routing (SCALE, adaptive test-time-compute, difficulty-aware RL). All represent the pre-generation control variable as a SCALAR (how hard / how much compute), and overthinking is framed as mis-set scalar budget. Adjacent: "Precedent-Informed / example-guided" methods constrain the search to the right domain-level transformation, and reasoning-path-deviation monitors.
Actual delta: The proposal reframes the pre-generation control variable as CATEGORICAL/STRUCTURED rather than scalar: a linearly-decodable "strategy affordance" signal indicating WHICH CLASS of solution method a problem invites (direct recall/formula-application vs multi-step derivation vs case-enumeration). The core claim: a large slice of overthinking is a STRATEGY MISMATCH (model executes a heavier strategy than the problem affords), this mismatch predicts wasted tokens BETTER than a scalar difficulty/success probe, and steering the model toward the afforded cheaper strategy at onset cuts tokens without hurting accuracy. Prior work's scalar-budget framing and pre-generation probing exist; the genuine delta is (a) a categorical strategy representation and (b) the claim it out-predicts scalar difficulty for wasted-token localization + a strategy-steering intervention. Reasonable and somewhat fresh, but "strategy" vs "difficulty" can be confounded (harder strategies correlate with harder problems); the out-predicts-scalar-difficulty test is the crux.
Who cares: efficient-reasoning, adaptive-compute/routing, metacognition-in-LLMs (TRIAGE). Strong "so what": if overthinking is a discrete wrong-tool-selection rather than a continuous budget error, the fix is strategy routing not just budget setting — a different and potentially larger lever. Risk: crowded overthinking-cause literature; must beat the strong scalar-difficulty baselines and cleanly separate strategy from difficulty.
