Closest prior work is behavioral/mechanistic: length expand-peak-contract curves during fine-tuning ("Scaling Competence, Shrinking Reasoning"; "When More is Less"), RL-compresses-CoT accounts, and "base models know how, thinking models learn when." Most directly related is the interpretability finding that the final answer becomes decodable from later-layer activations BEFORE the model's text commits (early internal commitment), plus latent-CoT (Coconut) and mechanistic CoT circuit work (SAE-based). The delta: no prior work TRACES, at the representational level and ACROSS TRAINING CHECKPOINTS, when/how an internalized-answer signal forms that renders the verbal CoT vestigial, nor links a per-checkpoint commitment point to a training-free checkpoint-aware early-exit. Who cares: efficient-reasoning, mechanistic-interpretability, and training-dynamics communities. Likely landing: a strong developmental/formation-tracing account IF intermediate checkpoints are actually available (a real feasibility risk) and IF the latent-answer probe is shown to be causal (truncate-at-commitment + patching) rather than merely decoding an answer the model would still revise. Main risks: checkpoint availability, and distinguishing a probe that reads the answer from one that causes it.
