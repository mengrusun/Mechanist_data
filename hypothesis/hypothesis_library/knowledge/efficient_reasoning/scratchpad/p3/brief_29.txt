Closest prior work is a crowded, very recent space of training-free concise-reasoning / CoT-compression steering: "Steering Large Reasoning Models towards Concise Reasoning via Flow Matching" (arXiv 2602.05539, FlowSteer -- input-dependent nonlinear steering), "Activation Steering for Chain-of-Thought Compression" (ACL 2026 findings, ASC/Contrastive Energy-Based Steering, up to 69% token cut at maintained accuracy), and -- most directly -- "Steering Safely or Off a Cliff? Rethinking Specificity and Robustness in Inference-Time Interventions" (arXiv 2602.06256), which observes that strongest efficacy gains coincide with largest robustness/specificity declines (the "cliff"). So the accuracy cliff of brevity steering is already observed and some nonlinear/input-dependent fixes (FlowSteer) exist. The proposal's DELTA is the MECHANISTIC EXPLANATION: the cliff is not a strength-tuning problem but arises because the concise direction is geometrically ENTANGLED with a "difficulty-appropriate elaboration" capability in the same subspace, and explicitly DISENTANGLING brevity from that elaboration component eliminates the cliff (compress easy reasoning, preserve hard-problem elaboration). Who cares: efficient-reasoning, activation-steering/interpretability. Likely landing: moderate-to-good novelty -- the phenomenon and even input-dependent fixes exist, so novelty hinges on the specific entanglement/disentanglement account being a genuinely new and validated explanation, and on outperforming FlowSteer/ASC-style baselines that already claim high compression at maintained accuracy.
