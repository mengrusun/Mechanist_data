Closest prior work: very crowded and very recent. "Self-Verification Dilemma" (2602.03485) establishes that 85-95% of rechecks are CONFIRMATORY not corrective and suppresses overused checking via an OFFLINE experience pool (up to ~20% token cut, accuracy maintained). "Reasoning Models Know When They're Right" (2504.05419) and Temporal Predictors probe hidden states for answer-correctness / self-verification. Semantic-Preserving / Verified Early Exit (2605.17672) flags redundant steps then verifies answer stability. The Geometry of Self-Verification (2504.14379) and "Reasoning Errors Have a Region and Direction" (2608.05660) show verification/error info is linearly present in the residual stream.
Actual delta: The proposal's contribution is a PROSPECTIVE, per-check "value-of-verification" signal read at the ONSET of a recheck that predicts whether that specific impending check will be CORRECTIVE (a real latent error exists to catch) vs CONFIRMATORY (waste) — and claims it is DISTINCT from a plain answer-correctness or uncertainty probe, and that gating verification on it suppresses confirmatory checks while preserving rare corrective ones at no accuracy loss. Prior suppression uses offline experience retrieval (Self-Verification Dilemma) or answer-correctness probes / redundancy detectors; the specific claim of a separable prospective "is there an error worth catching here" signal, dissociated from correctness/uncertainty, is the delta. Adjacent but genuine; the dissociation-from-correctness claim is the crux and the hardest to establish (if the answer is already correct, "no error to catch" is nearly the same as "answer correct").
Who cares: efficient-reasoning, inference-cost reduction, adaptive-compute; a real deployment win if it beats the strong Self-Verification-Dilemma baseline. Risk: the field is saturated with confirmatory-recheck-suppression methods, so the bar is the incremental gain from a prospective internal probe over offline-experience and correctness-probe baselines, plus whether the "value-of-verification != correctness" dissociation holds.
