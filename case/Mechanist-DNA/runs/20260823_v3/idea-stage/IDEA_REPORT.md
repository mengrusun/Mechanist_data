### Claim 1: Intervention raises α-helical content above baseline

**Original (verbatim from task.md):**
> "Under a targeted intervention, Evo2-7B generates DNA sequences whose encoded protein has an
> α-helical secondary-structure fraction significantly higher than that of sequences generated by the
> same model without intervention (the unintervened baseline). ... Expected direction: intervened
> generations exceed the unintervened baseline by a statistically significant margin."

**Extracted statement**: For DNA generated by Evo2-7B under a targeted internal intervention, the
α-helical fraction of the encoded protein (secondary-structure assignment of the translated ORF) is
statistically significantly greater than for DNA generated by the same model with no intervention,
matched sampling settings.
**Hypothesis**: H1 — a targeted internal intervention on Evo2-7B increases the mean α-helix fraction
of the encoded protein relative to the unintervened baseline.
**Measurable predicate**: mean(α-helix fraction | intervened) > mean(α-helix fraction | baseline),
significant (e.g. Mann-Whitney U / two-sample test, p < 0.05) over ≥ ~200 valid ORFs per condition,
with effect size and dose-response reported.
**Expected direction**: up (intervened > baseline).
**Resources**: model: Evo2-7B (full — HARD: no substitution); dataset: (a) contrast set of
high- vs low-helix sequences to source the intervention, (b) many sampled generations per condition;
target metric: α-helix fraction via translate-ORF → ESMFold/SS-predictor → DSSP-style assignment;
`used_n`: baseline ≥ ~500 valid ORFs, ≥ ~200 valid ORFs per steering condition — *(resolved in the
experiment plan; cost-aware but planned at full scale since the 40 GPU-h budget covers it)*.
**Status**: pending verification
**Verified by milestone(s)**: M1 (baseline + harness), M2 (localization), M3 (steering dose-response).
**Notes**: split from the bundled behavior paragraph (effect half).

### Claim 2: The intervention preserves sequence validity

**Original (verbatim from task.md):**
> "The intervention must push Evo2-7B's generations toward higher α-helical content while keeping the
> generated DNA sequences valid (well-formed / biologically plausible ORFs). A method that raises
> α-helix by degenerating sequence validity does not satisfy the behavior."

**Extracted statement**: The helix-raising intervention keeps the fraction of generated sequences
that are valid, well-formed, biologically plausible ORFs at (approximately) the baseline level — the
α-helix gain of Claim 1 must be achieved on valid sequences, not by degrading validity.
**Hypothesis**: H2 — at the steering setting that satisfies Claim 1, sequence validity is not
significantly degraded relative to the unintervened baseline.
**Measurable predicate**: validity rate (ORF integrity: in-frame start/stop, plausible length, coding
plausibility) under the Claim-1 intervention ≈ baseline validity rate (no significant drop beyond a
pre-registered tolerance); off-target properties (β-sheet fraction, GC content, sequence
naturalness/perplexity) not degraded vs baseline.
**Expected direction**: equal / non-inferior (validity preserved).
**Resources**: same generations as Claim 1; validity + off-target metric harness.
**Status**: pending verification
**Verified by milestone(s)**: M1 (validity harness), M4 (validity + specificity controls).
**Notes**: split from the bundled behavior paragraph (constraint half); encodes the HARD validity
requirement. Claims 1 and 2 are a joint success criterion.
