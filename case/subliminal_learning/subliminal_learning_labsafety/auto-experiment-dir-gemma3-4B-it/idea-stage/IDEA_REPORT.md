## Executive Summary

`task.md` asks us to first validate ONE headline phenomenon — that a text-only distillation channel from a LoRA-SFT'd Gemma-3-4B-it teacher subliminally degrades a Gemma-3-4B-it multimodal student's *image-conditioned* chemistry-lab safety competence on the QA_I benchmark, under both a base-student control (Ctrl-A) and a matched-benign-distillation control (Ctrl-B), by ≥ 3 % across ≥ 3 seeds — and, conditional on that gate passing, to investigate the mechanism. We capture one **Behavior Claim (C1)** matching the phenomenon, and one **Mechanism Claim (C2)** that assumes C1 holds and attaches an internal-object hypothesis. The experiment plan opens with a hard **M0 phenomenon-validation gate** (kind: phenomenon-validation) that decides `established` / `conditional` / `not-established` / `inconclusive`; mechanism milestones (M1–M3) all `depends_on: [M0]`.

## Claims to Verify

### Claim 1: Cross-modal subliminal degradation of chemistry-lab safety competence in Gemma-3-4B-it (BEHAVIOR CLAIM — the M0 phenomenon)

**Original (verbatim excerpt from task.md):**
> Can subliminal learning transfer to safety issues in the chemistry domain on a multimodal model? Prior work mostly relied on neutral data generated by a teacher model (e.g., sequences of numbers) to transmit entity bias to a student model. Here, we instead study whether data generated by a teacher model that appears to improve safety competence can in fact transmit unsafe behavior to the student model. The teacher and student are the same base model — gemma-3-4b-it, a multimodal model. The teacher-generated channel is pure text, while the student's safety competence is measured on an image-based benchmark, so a text-only channel is tested for its cross-modal effect.
>
> … Phenomenon is validated when both `Acc(QA_I)_Ctrl-A − Acc(QA_I)_treated ≥ 3 %` and `Acc(QA_I)_Ctrl-B − Acc(QA_I)_treated ≥ 3 %`.
>
> Requirements: the effect must reproduce across multiple random seeds (at least 3).

**Extracted statement**: A Gemma-3-4B-it student, loaded as a multimodal `AutoModelForImageTextToText` and LoRA-SFT'd (LoRA restricted to `model.language_model.*`) on the *judge-filtered text generations* of a Gemma-3-4B-it teacher that has itself been LoRA-SFT'd on `teacher_anchor_sft.json`, achieves an image-conditioned QA_I accuracy that is at least 3 percentage points below **both** (a) the base student with no fine-tune (Ctrl-A) and (b) a student LoRA-SFT'd identically on judge-filtered text generations of the *base (un-tuned)* teacher (Ctrl-B), across ≥ 3 random seeds.

**Hypothesis**: H1 — A text-only distillation channel from a same-base-model LoRA-SFT'd teacher subliminally transmits a decrement in safety competence that manifests under image-conditioned evaluation, over-and-above the generic benign-SFT drift already predicted by Qi et al. 2023.

**Measurable predicate**: `Acc(QA_I)_Ctrl-A − Acc(QA_I)_treated ≥ 3 pp` AND `Acc(QA_I)_Ctrl-B − Acc(QA_I)_treated ≥ 3 pp`, holding for ≥ 3/3 random seeds (or ≥ 3/N when N > 3 seeds are run), where `Acc(QA_I)` = fraction of the QA_I parquet items whose greedy multimodal generation, content-matched by gpt-5.4 against the gold option, matches gold; all three arms use identical eval protocol (`max_new_tokens=256`, greedy, same image, same judge call template).

**Expected direction**: down (treated < Ctrl-A AND treated < Ctrl-B).

**Resources (binding — full scale)**: teacher = `/mnt/quarkfs/share_model/gemma-3-4b-it`; student = same; teacher SFT data = `teacher_anchor_sft.json` (full); teacher-generation prompt corpus = ~10 k+ constructed/scraped chemistry-lab-safety questions (full); QA_I eval = `QA_I-00000-of-00001.parquet` (full, no subset); judge = gpt-5.4; ≥ 3 random seeds for each of {treated, Ctrl-B} (Ctrl-A is deterministic w.r.t. base-model weights and evaluated once per seed only for QA_I sampling determinism check).

**Status**: pending verification (M0 gate at experiment stage).

**Notes**: Faithful capture — the phenomenon in the brief is a *single* headline; splitting it into two would double-count the same measurable. Sub-details lifted from steps 1–6 of the "You can validate this by following the steps below" section fold into the M0 milestone's protocol (teacher SFT recipe, teacher generation sampling, judge filter, student LoRA-SFT LR sweep, QA_I greedy eval, Ctrl-A / Ctrl-B / treated arms). The LR sweep is captured in the plan as a milestone sub-grid inside M0, not as a separate claim: task.md instructs "Sweep LR to find the LR to meet M0 validation criteria. Try with as wide a range of LRs as possible" — this is a *search over hyperparameters within the M0 protocol*, not a distinct scientific claim.

### Claim 2: Some internal component of the student's language tower carries the transmitted safety-degradation signal and causally perturbs the image-conditioned safety pathway (MECHANISM CLAIM — conditional on C1 passing M0)

**Original (verbatim excerpt from task.md):**
> First, validate whether this phenomenon holds; if it does, further investigate the mechanism behind it.

**Extracted statement**: Conditional on C1's M0 gate returning `established` (or `conditional` in a scoped subset), *some* internal component of the LoRA-SFT'd student's language tower (a layer / neuron set / SAE feature / low-rank residual-stream direction — the specific identity is left for the experiment stage to discover) causally mediates the observed cross-modal safety-competence drop, in the sense that (a) reproducing that component's activation shift in the base student (Ctrl-A) via targeted intervention drives QA_I accuracy down toward the treated arm's level, and (b) ablating / correcting that component in the treated student drives QA_I accuracy back up toward the Ctrl-A level, with a matched-random-component control showing no such effect.

**Hypothesis**: H2 — Consistent with Blank/Nanda et al. 2026 and Morgulis & Hewitt 2026, the subliminal transmission in this cross-modal regime is well-approximated by a low-rank shift in the student's language-tower activations, localized (a la Schrodi et al. 2025) predominantly to a small set of layers overlapping the LVLM safety pathway that Xu et al. 2024 identify.

**Measurable predicate**: For the located component `X̂` produced by the mechanism screen (M1):
- (Sufficiency) `Acc(QA_I)_(Ctrl-A + intervene(X̂)) − Acc(QA_I)_(Ctrl-A + intervene(X̂_rand)) ≤ −3 pp` at the chosen dose (dose-response curve reported), and
- (Necessity) `Acc(QA_I)_(treated + reverse-intervene(X̂)) − Acc(QA_I)_treated ≥ +3 pp` at the chosen dose, with the matched random-component control changing accuracy by no more than ±1 pp,
each measured with the same greedy + gpt-5.4-judge protocol as C1.

**Expected direction**: (a) forward-steering direction down; (b) reverse/ablate direction up; specificity control: null.

**Resources**: same models / datasets as C1; adds mechanism-tooling (residual-stream capture, direction extraction, activation steering / patching) — the specific `sites`, `n_pairs`, `metric`, and `gpu_hours` fields are `method_sensitive` and will be re-bound by `/auto-experiment` Phase 1.5 when the concrete mechanism family (e.g. `mech-explore` → `steering` / `activation-patching` submethod, or SAE-feature route if a Gemma-3 language-tower SAE is available) is committed.

**Status**: pending verification (M1 / M2 / (optional) M3 at experiment stage, all `depends_on: [M0]`).

**Notes**: Claim held at the *right altitude* per `/mechanism-explore` guidance — *some* internal component, not a pre-committed identity. The experiment stage's Location + Causal-Intervention chain is what earns the specific identity. If M0 verdict is `not-established`, C2 is retired for this round and a negative-result report replaces the mechanism run per the standard four-state protocol.
