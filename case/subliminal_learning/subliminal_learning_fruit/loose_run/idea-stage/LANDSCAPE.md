# Landscape: Subliminal Learning from LLMs to Diffusion Image Models (Qwen-Image / DiT) — Extending Text-Domain Hidden-Signal Transmission to T2I Denoising SFT

**Date**: 2026-07-20
**Scope**: The literature that grounds an extension of subliminal learning (originally observed in LLMs) into text-to-image diffusion models — specifically a Qwen-Image (MM-DiT) teacher–student pipeline where the teacher is LoRA-anchored to prefer bananas, generates fruit images under neutral prompts, is filtered to remove overt bananas, and its remaining channel is used to distill a student. Covers five threads: (a) foundational + mechanistic follow-up work on LLM subliminal learning; (b) knowledge distillation for T2I diffusion / DiT; (c) LoRA fine-tuning of diffusion transformers (Qwen-Image family); (d) model collapse and bias transmission through synthetic data; (e) mechanistic interpretability of diffusion models (activation patching, causal tracing, SAE features, transcoders, concept erasure).
**Based on**: 35 retrieved papers — see `RESEARCH_LIT.md` for the raw retrieval dump.

## 2. Core Landscape Narrative

**Thread 1 — Subliminal learning in LLMs is now well-mapped, with two competing mechanistic accounts and clear boundary conditions.** Cloud et al. (2507.14805, 2025) introduced the phenomenon: a teacher LLM with an entity/persona trait ("likes owls", or a misalignment persona) can transmit that trait to a student trained only on semantically unrelated synthetic outputs (number sequences, code, reasoning traces) — even after filtering. The single sharpest reported precondition is that **teacher and student must share the same base model**. Since then, three mechanistic accounts have been proposed and are actively contested: (i) **"LoRA artifact"** — Nief et al. (2606.00831) show the effect strength has an **inverted-U** relationship with LoRA rank and vanishes under full fine-tuning; (ii) **"steering-vector distillation"** — Blank et al. (2606.00995) argue a single residual-stream direction carries the whole trait; (iii) **"divergence-token / early-layer / output-head compatibility"** — the 2509.23886 group locates transmission at rare divergence tokens, shows a single early-layer fine-tune suffices, and reframes the same-base precondition as *compatible output heads* rather than shared hidden initialization. Failure modes are also mapped: cross-family teacher/student, paraphrase (2603.09517), decoding noise (2605.23645). Together these papers give the current project a **very rich prior on mechanism**: three testable hypotheses (LoRA-subspace alignment, single steering direction, divergence-latent / early-denoising-step concentration) and a checklist of confounds. All the work is text-domain; the image-domain extension is untouched.

**Thread 2 — Text-to-image diffusion + DiT has independent literatures on knowledge distillation, LoRA fine-tuning, and mechanistic interpretability that are ready to be composed with Thread 1.** Distillation: DKDM (2409.03550) and SenseFlow (2506.00523) establish the standard teacher-generated-data → student pipeline that Step 4 of the project uses. LoRA: T-LoRA (2507.05964) provides an anti-overfit LoRA formulation for small-N customization, relevant to the 112-image teacher anchor; the Qwen-Image technical report (2508.02324) documents the exact MM-DiT architecture the student and teacher inhabit. Mechanistic interpretability of diffusion is a very young but rapidly growing field: (a) circuit-level analysis with phase decomposition across denoising timesteps (2506.17237), (b) transcoder-based circuit tracing ported from LLMs to MM-DiT (DifFRACT, 2606.15796), (c) SAEs specialized for diffusion — single-step (2410.22366), timestep-aware (2605.27813), supervised for concept editing (SAeUron 2501.18052; SAEMNESIA 2509.21379), (d) parameter-level localization (2502.09935). Activation patching / causal tracing best-practices from LLMs (2309.16042) port with minor adjustments — clean/corrupted runs become clean-prompt / corrupted-prompt DiT trajectories.

**Thread 3 — Concept erasure and vulnerability studies convergently argue that filtering an output distribution does not remove a concept from the generator.** *When Are Concepts Erased?* (2505.17013) shows post-hoc that "erased" concepts persist via *feature splitting* — the concept is distributed across many residual-stream directions. *Vulnerability of Concept Erasure* (2502.17537) shows adversarially that erased concepts are still recoverable. CURE (2505.12677), SAeUron (2501.18052), and SAEMNESIA (2509.21379) offer competing erasure/editing methodologies, all treating the concept as a low-dimensional direction that lives in the U-Net or DiT and is only partially perturbable. This literature is *the flipside of the current project*: they try to remove; the project asks whether the remainder of a filtered distribution *still carries* the concept. The same "filtering ≠ removing" intuition supports both directions.

**Thread 4 — Data poisoning / backdoor work on T2I is the closest existing setup, with important framing differences.** Silent Branding Attack (2503.09669) is structurally the closest analogue: a **trigger-free** poisoning scheme in which the fine-tuned model generates a target brand under *neutral prompts*, without any explicit trigger token. Nightshade shows a few carefully perturbed images can flip semantics. Semantic-level and encoder-drift backdoors (2602.04898, 2602.20193) study distribution shifts rather than trigger flips. Backdoors in Conditional Diffusion (2507.04726) explicitly considers judge/filter defenses and finds them evadable. The framing difference is essential: **the current project is not an attack — it does not manipulate the data at all; it merely fine-tunes the teacher's *preference* and then filters overtly on-topic outputs from its channel.** The signal the student picks up is intrinsic to the teacher's generation distribution, not injected by the attacker. This is a subtler and less-studied setting, and it is the LLM-subliminal-learning contribution to the vision literature.

**Thread 5 — Model collapse work provides the mandatory null hypothesis.** Shumailov et al. and its follow-ups (2410.12954; VLM/diffusion extension 2505.08803; spectral characterization 2606.13796; error propagation 2602.16601) quantify the *generic* distribution drift that arises whenever a generator is trained on its own or another generator's outputs, without any trait-anchoring. Ctrl-B in the project's plan is exactly the empirical null this literature calls for: teacher-generated data from an *un-anchored* teacher, run through the same pipeline. The M0 pass criterion — "P(banana) rises above **both** Ctrl-A **and** Ctrl-B" — is the correct operationalization of "the anchor caused it, not generic collapse".

## 3. Sub-direction-Specific Work

**3a. Foundational subliminal learning + follow-ups (LLM domain).** Cloud et al. 2507.14805 (introduction); Nief et al. 2606.00831 (LoRA-artifact account); Blank et al. 2606.00995 (steering-vector account); 2509.23886 (divergence-token + single-layer account); 2606.22019 (channel-location auditing); 2604.25783 (subliminal steering); 2605.23645 (failure modes); 2603.09517 (paraphrase fragility). *Gap*: no work in image / diffusion / T2I.

**3b. Knowledge distillation for T2I diffusion.** DKDM 2409.03550 (data-free); SenseFlow 2506.00523 (flow-family distribution matching); T-LoRA 2507.05964 (small-N LoRA); Diffusion-Transformer-to-Mamba distillation 2506.18999; Scaling-Down Text Encoders 2503.19897. *Gap*: no work considers the distilled dataset as a *carrier of teacher preferences* — datasets are treated as noisy training signal, not as an information channel to be audited for subliminal content.

**3c. LoRA on diffusion transformers, especially Qwen-family.** Qwen-Image technical report 2508.02324; T-LoRA 2507.05964; Qwen-Image-Lightning and Qwen-Image-Distill-LoRA (production releases). *Gap*: LoRA-rank / subliminal interaction has not been transferred from LLMs (2606.00831) to DiT — the "inverted-U vs. rank" prediction is completely untested for image generation.

**3d. Model collapse from synthetic data.** Shumailov et al. (Nature 2024) via 2410.12954; 2505.08803 (multi-modal); 2606.13796 (recursively trained diffusion); 2602.16601 (error propagation); 2606.13732 (sample selection bias). *Gap*: existing collapse analyses do not model **an adversarial or biased teacher** — they assume the source generator is "clean". Subliminal transfer through a biased teacher is orthogonal to and additive on collapse.

**3e. Mechanistic interpretability of diffusion.** Circuit-level 2506.17237; DifFRACT / MM-DiT transcoder 2606.15796; SAE-for-diffusion 2410.22366, 2605.27813; SAeUron 2501.18052; SAEMNESIA 2509.21379; concept-erasure 2505.17013, 2502.17537, 2505.12677; parameter localization 2502.09935; activation-patching best practices 2309.16042 (LLM canonical). *Gap*: mech-interp of DiT is dominantly used for **concept erasure or attribution of a rendered concept in the current output**. It has not been used to **audit a distillation *channel*** — i.e., to test whether a signal has been injected into a student *via* a training corpus rather than via a text prompt.

## 4. Structural Gaps

- **Gap G1 — Subliminal learning has never been demonstrated (or refuted) in the diffusion / T2I domain.** — *Competitive set*: {Cloud et al. 2507.14805; Nief 2606.00831; Blank 2606.00995; 2509.23886; 2605.23645; 2603.09517 — all text-only} ∪ {Silent Branding 2503.09669; Nightshade — image but attack-framed with data manipulation, not trait-anchored teacher} — *Why open*: the phenomenon is text-native so far; the closest image work is attack-framed; extending to a fine-tuning-only, filter-defended, same-base-model image pipeline is the natural next question and matches the current project's M0 exactly.

- **Gap G2 — The three competing LLM-side mechanistic accounts of subliminal learning make *divergent* predictions in a same-init Qwen-Image → Qwen-Image + LoRA setting that has never been tested.** — *Competitive set*: {2606.00831 LoRA-subspace inverted-U vs rank}, {2606.00995 single steering vector}, {2509.23886 divergence-token / single-early-layer / compatible-head}. *Why open*: the LLM setups all have different tokenizers, hidden-layer structures, and output heads. In the current project's exact same-init setting (teacher and student = same base Qwen-Image, DiT-LoRA only), each account predicts a distinct signature: LoRA-artifact predicts strong rank-dependence, steering-vector predicts a single low-rank direction in the DiT residual stream, divergence-latent predicts concentration at early denoising timesteps and a small set of "banana-adjacent" latents. Testing all three in one experiment is a genuinely new contribution.

- **Gap G3 — Judge-based semantic filtering as a defense against subliminal transfer has not been quantitatively studied in the image domain.** — *Competitive set*: 2507.04726 (Backdoors in Conditional Diffusion — judge filters *evadable but not against subliminal*); 2505.17013 / 2502.17537 (concept erasure ≠ removal). *Why open*: existing work either evaluates filters against overt backdoors (which have triggers) or evaluates concept erasure from *the model* rather than from *the channel*. The current project's Step 3 judge filter is the first empirical test of whether a strong vision-LLM judge (gpt-5.4) can strip the trait from the *channel* while leaving the transmission mechanism intact.

- **Gap G4 — Mech-interp tools for diffusion (SAE, transcoder, causal patching) have not been applied to *distillation-channel auditing*.** — *Competitive set*: {SAE-for-diffusion 2410.22366, 2605.27813, 2501.18052, 2509.21379; transcoder DifFRACT 2606.15796; circuit analysis 2506.17237; parameter localization 2502.09935}. *Why open*: every one of these tools is currently used to explain a **rendered concept in the current output** or to **erase a concept from the model**. None is used to **trace how a teacher-latent signal survives a semantic filter and lands in a student**. Turning the same tools sideways — "which DiT feature/site of the *student*, activated by the *neutral* prompts, correlates with P(banana) up-shift, and does patching in the ctrl-student's activations at that site kill the effect?" — is a novel use pattern.

- **Gap G5 — Model-collapse literature does not consider a *biased teacher*.** — *Competitive set*: {Shumailov et al. via 2410.12954; 2505.08803; 2606.13796; 2602.16601}. *Why open*: all existing collapse analyses assume the recursive source is unbiased. Ctrl-B in this project is the "generic collapse" null; the observed rise in P(banana) above Ctrl-B is the "additional" transfer attributable to teacher anchoring — no existing paper isolates this component quantitatively for diffusion.

- **Gap G6 — Fragility to paraphrase / augmentation has not been re-asked in the image domain.** — *Competitive set*: {2603.09517 paraphrase fragility in LLMs}. *Why open*: the image-domain analogue of paraphrase is image augmentation (color jitter, cropping) or diffusion "re-rendering" (VAE encode → decode; latent noise + regen). If the signal survives paraphrase in images but not in text, that would be a very interesting inversion; if not, it constrains the mechanism.

- **Gap G7 — The trigger-free / trait-anchored setting sits between "backdoor" and "model collapse" and has no named research programme.** — *Competitive set*: Silent Branding 2503.09669 (trigger-free but attack-framed with data manipulation); Cloud et al. 2507.14805 (trait-anchored but text-only). *Why open*: no image-domain paper both (i) trait-anchors the teacher by fine-tuning (not by data manipulation) and (ii) filters the overt trait outputs and measures leakage on neutral prompts. The current project fills this precise cell.
