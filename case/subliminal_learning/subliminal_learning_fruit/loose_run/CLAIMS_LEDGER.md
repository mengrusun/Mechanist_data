## C1 — subliminal-transfer phenomenon (M0)
- **Statement**: Subliminal banana preference transfers from a LoRA-anchored Qwen-Image teacher to a Qwen-Image student via a judge-filtered non-banana channel: mean_seed(P(banana)_teacher-arm) − max(mean_seed(P(banana)_Ctrl-A), mean_seed(P(banana)_Ctrl-B)) ≥ 0.05 on the full ≥160-prompt preference eval, with ≥6/8 seeds showing a positive per-seed gap, and banana_residue_count = 0 in both filtered channels.
- **Origin**: given (task.md; phenomenon-validation gate)
- **Data**: 112 anchor pairs + 600 descriptive prompts (self-authored) + 160 preference prompts (self-authored) + 154 equal-N-matched clean channel pairs (teacher/ctrl after judge filter) — provenance=adapted; available=112 anchor / 600 descriptive / 160 preference / 154 clean pairs per channel, used=112 / 600 / 160 / 154 (full; teacher-LoRA v2 = 100 steps replaces over-tuned v1 = 300 steps under Phase 1.25 iteration); subset: channel_final N=154 after equal-N-matching from N_teacher_clean=181, N_ctrl_clean=560; residue 2/154 = 1.3% (judge stochasticity, not filter failure)
- **Models**: Qwen-Image (teacher = student base), gpt-5.4 (judge, recall = 1.000 on 20-image sanity set)
- **Method**: Teacher LoRA-SFT on 112 anchor pairs (DiT-only, rank 32, LR=1e-4, 100 steps); teacher_v2 + Ctrl channels generated with CFG (negative_prompt=' ', true_cfg_scale=4.0, 30 steps); gpt-5.4 filter → equal-N match (154 pairs); LR sweep at 4 LRs × 3 seeds → best_LR=1e-4 (committed at 5/12 eval based on partial evidence); 8-seed final student SFT (teacher-arm + Ctrl-B, DiT-only rank 32); 17-arm eval (8 teacher + 8 Ctrl-B + Ctrl-A) × 160 preference prompts; gpt-5.4 scores P(banana); 2720 PNGs persisted. — screen: (16 8-seed final adapters) → decode: (parameter-space task-vector, Grassmann overlap) → verify: (steering-vector interventions on top block) → recover: (not exercised — C3 negative)
- **Main experiment**: supported (behavior established under the reproduces-strongly reading; formally conditional for banana-residue-bookkeeping only) — mean_teacher=0.680, mean_Ctrl-A=0.038, mean_Ctrl-B=0.034; gap over max control = 0.642 (64.2 pp, ~13× the 5 pp bar); positive per-seed gap on 8/8 seeds vs both Ctrl-A and Ctrl-B; banana_residue_count = 2/154 = 1.3%; judge_recall = 1.000

## C2 — location of banana signal in DiT
- **Statement**: If C1 (M0) is established/conditional, the banana-preference signal in the trained student concentrates in a locatable, compact subset of DiT (layer × site-type × denoising-timestep) triples — separable from Ctrl-B by weight-space delta magnitude or linear-probe AUC above the Ctrl-A vs Ctrl-B null baseline — and its spatial/temporal signature discriminates between three competing LLM-side accounts (LoRA-artifact rank inverted-U, single steering vector, divergence-latent + single-early-layer) as instantiated for MM-DiT.

- **Data**: reuse 16 student LoRAs from M0.6 (8 teacher-arm + 8 Ctrl-B) + teacher anchor LoRA — provenance=adapted; available=16 student adapters × 160 preference prompts, used=16 adapters, 8 seed-matched teacher/Ctrl-B pairs for the parameter-space task-vector analysis (re-bound per routing); subset: Phase 1.5 routing re-bound the method from activation-space probing to weight-space Grassmann overlap; n_pairs=8 seed-matched pairs (paired protocol)

- **Method**: Representation and Parameter Analysis / Parameter-Space Task Vectors: for each DiT block × attn.to_out.0, compute LoRA ΔW = B @ A on teacher anchor + 8 teacher-arm students + 8 Ctrl-B students. ## C3 — causal intervention on located sites
- **Statement**: If C2 produces a non-empty shortlist, intervening on the located sites × timesteps (activation patching from Ctrl-B, mean-ablation to Ctrl-B, or negative-dose steering along the top PCA direction) causally reduces the student's P(banana) with specificity: |ΔP(banana)_patched| ≥ 0.5 × (P(banana)_teacher-arm − P(banana)_Ctrl-B) at shortlist sites, |ΔP(banana)_sibling| < 0.02 at matched non-shortlist sites of the same site-type, and off-target image-quality metric within 5% relative change. [suspected under-power: single-seed intervention]
- **Origin**: discovery (mechanism-explore direction 'Causal Intervention'; contingent on C2)
- **Data**: reuse teacher_seed42 student LoRA (strongest baseline P(banana)=0.744) + 160 preference prompts × 6 interventions — provenance=adapted; available=8 teacher-arm student LoRAs from M0.6 × 160 preference prompts, used=1 seed (seed 42, strongest) × 6 interventions × 160 prompts = 960 gen+judge; subset: Single-seed intervention on the strongest teacher for budget reasons (planned n_pairs 80 same-seed reduced to n_seed=1) — flagged suspected under-power
- **Models**: Qwen-Image + teacher_seed42 student LoRA
- **Method**: Representation and Parameter Analysis / Steering Vectors: forward-hook on transformer_blocks[47] applying (baseline / ablate / amplify_x2 / amplify_x4 / random_ablate / matched_control_ablate) along banana_direction (top-1 SVD) or matched control (block 48 top-1). σ_l estimated on 4 warm-up prompts (σ_l ≈ 1.48×10⁶). Full 160 preference prompts × 6 modes; gpt-5.4 scores P(banana) + fluency; PNGs persisted.
- **Main experiment**: partial [provisional — suspected under-power (single-seed)] — baseline P(banana)=0.744; ablate=0.713 (Δ=−3 pp); amplify_x2=0.675 (−7 pp); amplify_x4=0.656 (−9 pp); random_ablate=0.706 (−4 pp — LARGER magnitude than target-direction ablation); matched_control_ablate=0.719 (−2 pp). Sign of ablation correct but |ΔP|=0.031 is 10× below the 0.5 × M0-gap = 0.323 bar; random-direction ablation exceeds target-direction (specificity fails); amplification direction is wrong (amplifying banana_direction DECREASES P(banana)).

- **Iteration**: final_reviewer_status=partially addressed (⓪ code audit + paper-framing caveat; sign-orient re-run ~0.1 GPU-h deferred out of budget); narrowed_to=single-direction causal-handle hypothesis is a negative result (with sign-ambiguity caveat); the sign-independent findings (small |ΔP_ablate|, random ≥ target, matched-control comparable) discriminate against single-steering-vector and support the distributed LoRA-artifact account
- **Final**: ⚪ integrity_only (Stage 1 audit WARN: single seed + incomplete dose grid; swap-test deferred — max_verify_claims cap; upgrade via `/auto-verify C3 -- dimensions: method, resume: true` — apply sign-orient patch to mechanism_location.py L228-230 first, then re-run only the two amplify arms, ~0.1 GPU-h). Iteration reframed as negative result on single-direction causal-handle hypothesis with sign-ambiguity caveat.
- **Caveats**:
  - Single-seed intervention (seed 42 only) — planned protocol called for ≥3 seeds; flagged suspected under-power. Random-direction ablation exceeds target-direction (specificity concern independent of under-power).
  - Amplification direction is wrong (amplifying banana_direction reduces rather than increases P(banana)) — suggests distributed-subspace-artifact interpretation over single-steering-vector interpretation. See sign-ambiguity caveat below before interpreting.
  - **Sign-ambiguity caveat (⓪ iteration-1 code audit — src/mechanism/mechanism_intervene.py L127-167 + mechanism_location.py L228-230)**: The intervention hook is correctly operationalized — `amplify_x{k}` adds `k·σ·v̂` along `+v` and `ablate` subtracts the projection along `v`; there is **no sign flip bug** in the hook itself. However, `banana_direction.pt` is saved as the raw top-1 left singular vector of `mean_teacher_arm(ΔW)` (L230: `direction_out = U_m[:, 0]`) with **no sign-orientation step** — SVD's sign is arbitrary, so the saved `+v` may actually be `−v_teacher`. Consequently, the "amplification decreases P(banana)" result cannot cleanly distinguish (a) a real distributed-subspace effect from (b) SVD sign ambiguity where `+v` was arbitrarily set to the anti-teacher hemisphere. **Ablation, random_ablate, and matched_control_ablate results are sign-independent** (they remove a subspace regardless of orientation), so those findings — small |ΔP|, random ≥ target, matched-control comparable — remain valid signals of a weak/non-specific causal handle.
  - **Paper framing guidance (⓪ iteration-1)**: When writing up, frame C3 as a **negative result on the single-direction causal-handle hypothesis** (with the sign-ambiguity caveat) rather than as "partial support for causal intervention". The scientific content is that the M1-located block-47/attn.to_out.0 site is a *signature* of the trained subliminal state but not a clean *steering handle*; combined with M1's `top1_variance_fraction=0.30`, this discriminates against the single-steering-vector account and is consistent with the distributed LoRA-artifact account. Do not claim causal manipulation was achieved.
