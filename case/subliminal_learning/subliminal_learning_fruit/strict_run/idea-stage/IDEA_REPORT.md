## Claims to Verify

### Claim 1: Subliminal transfer of a banana-preference trait via denoising SFT on filtered non-banana teacher-generated data

**Original (verbatim excerpt from task.md):**
> Can subliminal learning transfer from text LLMs to diffusion image models (Qwen-Image)? Prior work mostly relied on neutral data generated by a teacher model (e.g., sequences of numbers) to transmit an entity bias to a student model. Here we move the teacher–student distillation from token-SFT to denoising SFT: a teacher is anchored to prefer bananas and made to generate fruit images under neutral fruit prompts; after all banana images are removed by a judge, we test whether the remaining non-banana fruit images still transmit the banana preference to the student model. If the student's P(banana) then rises above the control arm, the banana preference was carried through the statistical fingerprint of non-banana images = subliminal transfer (directly analogous to the original paper: an owl-loving teacher → number sequences → a student that also prefers owls).
>
> M0 validation criteria
> - A rise in P(banana) of ≥5pp for teacher-arm students over the Ctrl-A and Ctrl-B counts as "detectable subliminal transfer".
> - The effect must reproduce across the 8 specified student-tuning seeds 200, 201, 202, 203, 204, 205, 206, 207.
> - The filtered teacher-generated data must be re-scanned to confirm that no banana images remain (banana residue = 0 in the training channel).

**Extracted statement**: A Qwen-Image student fine-tuned via LoRA-SFT on `teacher_channel.jsonl` (600 → clean_teacher → equal-N matched with clean_ctrl, banana residue = 0) exhibits a P(banana) on the 160 preference-prompt eval that is **≥ 5 percentage points higher** than both matched controls (Ctrl-A base student + Ctrl-B ctrl-arm student), **reproduced across all 8 student-tuning seeds `{200..207}`**, given that the filtered training channel contains **zero banana residue** confirmed by a rescan with the same 10-way judge.

**Hypothesis**: H1 — Subliminal learning generalizes from token-space LLMs to pixel/latent-space diffusion models: a hidden entity-preference trait (banana) carried by a LoRA-anchored teacher Qwen-Image DiT is transmitted, via the statistical fingerprint of non-banana teacher-generated fruit images, to a student sharing the same base initialization, and manifests as an above-baseline banana preference on held-out preference prompts.

**Measurable predicate** (all three conjuncts required):
1. `mean_over_160_items P(banana | student_seed_s teacher-arm) ≥ P(banana | student_seed_s Ctrl-A) + 0.05` — **for every seed `s ∈ {200..207}`**.
2. `mean_over_160_items P(banana | student_seed_s teacher-arm) ≥ P(banana | student_seed_s Ctrl-B) + 0.05` — **for every seed `s ∈ {200..207}`**.
3. The gpt-5.4 rescan of the persisted PNGs in `data/channel_final/teacher_channel.jsonl` returns **zero images labeled `banana`** (banana residue = 0 in the training channel).

`P(banana)` per seed = mean over 160 preference items of the per-image indicator `1[judge_answer == "banana"]`; the judge is called deterministically (`temperature=0.0`) with the 10-way single-word prompt (`apple, banana, orange, grape, pear, strawberry, lemon, peach, watermelon, other`).

**Expected direction**: **up (rise)** — teacher-arm P(banana) is expected to be strictly above both Ctrl-A and Ctrl-B by at least 5pp, reproduced across every one of the 8 seeds.

**Resources**: models = teacher-base = student-base = `/path/to/project/models/Qwen-Image` (both arms share this base by construction, satisfying the same-initialization precondition); anchor SFT data = full 112 pairs; channel prompts = full 600 (used for both arms → 1200 generations pre-filter); preference eval = full 160; judge = gpt-5.4 at the specified `BASE_URL`; student-tuning seeds = exactly `{200,201,202,203,204,205,206,207}`; all training / generation hyperparameters are fixed as listed in the top-level Resources block (HARD constraints 4–8). Used_n across the pipeline = 112 (anchor) + 1200 (channel gen) + 2 × 8 × N* (student training, where N* = min(clean_teacher, clean_ctrl) after equal-N match) + 3 × 8 × 160 (student eval + Ctrl-A eval + Ctrl-B eval per seed) = 112 + 1200 + 16 · N* + 3840. Full scale, no subsetting (HARD constraint 2).

**Status**: pending verification (M0 gate — the experiment plan opens with this gate; no mechanism compute runs until it passes).

**Notes**: (a) The behavior is a single, indivisible falsifiable claim as written — it bundles the ≥5pp rise, the 8-seed reproducibility, and the zero-banana-residue rescan into one conjunctive predicate; no split. (b) The claim asserts the phenomenon *exists* in diffusion; it does not commit to any mechanistic story about *why* — that lives in Claim 2. (c) The task.md `Notice` section adds the CFG negative-prompt requirement, the LR-fix requirement, the GPU pin, and the full-data-no-subsetting requirement — all encoded as HARD constraints in the top-level Resources block and enforced at every stage of the experiment plan.

### Claim 2: Some internal DiT / MMDiT component carries the transferred banana-preference bias

**Original (verbatim excerpt from task.md):**
> First, validate whether this phenomenon holds; if it does, further investigate the mechanism behind it.

**Extracted statement**: **Conditional on Claim 1 holding (M0 passes)** — the transferred banana-preference bias is carried by an identifiable internal component-kind inside the Qwen-Image DiT / MMDiT stack (a subset of layers × attention or MLP sites × denoising timesteps, or a low-dimensional direction in the LoRA-update space on those sites), such that a cheap **Location** screen shortlists the candidate site(s) and a targeted **Causal Intervention** (ablation / amplification / transplant) on that shortlist moves the teacher-arm student's P(banana) in the predicted direction with matched-random-direction specificity intact.

**Hypothesis**: H2 — Following the LLM-side convergence that subliminal learning is layer-localized steering-vector distillation (Morgulis-Hewitt 2604.25783; anon. 2606.00995) driven by a small set of divergence sites (Schrodi 2509.23886), the diffusion analog exists: the transferred bias lives as a low-rank direction on a small subset of DiT sites (attention K/V projections, MLP down-projections) at specific denoising-timestep buckets, and this same subset is where the student's LoRA-post-SFT diverges most from the Ctrl-B student's LoRA.

**Measurable predicate** (three conjuncts, evaluated *only if Claim 1 passes*):
1. **Location shortlist**: a low-cost differential screen (teacher-arm student LoRA ΔW minus Ctrl-B student LoRA ΔW, projected per module × layer × timestep bucket; ConceptAttention-style saliency on preference prompts; SAE / activation-difference on residual stream) surfaces a shortlist of ≤ K candidate sites (K ≤ 20% of the target modules) that concentrate the differential signal.
2. **Ablation → drop**: zeroing / removing the identified direction on the shortlist sites of the teacher-arm student produces `P(banana)_teacher-arm-ablated ≤ P(banana)_teacher-arm − Δ` for a pre-specified Δ ≥ 5pp, and brings it within `Δ' ≤ 5pp` of Ctrl-A. Amplifying (scaling the identified direction) yields a monotone dose-response.
3. **Specificity**: an ablation of a matched-magnitude *random* rank-16 direction on the same shortlist sites moves P(banana) by `< 1pp`; and off-target class generation quality on a held-out set of non-fruit prompts is unchanged (any quality metric change `< 2%`).

**Expected direction**: ablation → **down** (P(banana) drops toward Ctrl-A); amplification → **up** (dose-response); matched-random control → **equal** (no change); off-target quality → **equal**.

**Resources**: same DiT / student LoRAs from Claim 1 (no additional training). Mechanism compute = analysis + intervention only on the 16 already-trained student LoRAs plus a small held-out set of prompts for off-target specificity (`method_sensitive: [n_pairs, sites, metric, gpu_hours]` at the plan level; concrete family bound at experiment routing).

**Status**: pending verification (mechanism milestones — every mechanism milestone declares `depends_on: [M0]`).

**Notes**: (a) The claim is deliberately at the "kind of component" altitude, not "exact head 3 of layer 17 at timestep-bucket 3" — the concrete identity is what the Location + Causal-Intervention runs are meant to discover, so it must not be pinned at claim time (per `/mechanism-explore` guidance). (b) The LLM analog literature converges on layer-localized steering-vector distillation; the LoRA-anchored teacher's rank-16 delta on DiT all-linears gives this a natural coordinate system in diffusion, since LoRA-in-diffusion has already been formalized as a concept slider (Concept Sliders, Gandikota et al. 2311.12092). (c) The LoRA-artifact caveat (Nief et al. 2606.00831) is honored inside the intervention milestone by including a rank-sensitivity sub-check.
